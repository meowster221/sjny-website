Family Owned and Operated

SJNY Construction is a father-and-son general contracting firm. Sunny builds, Danny manages, and both of them answer the phone.

How We Work

SJNY Construction is a licensed and fully insured general contractor serving New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. Every employee completes OSHA safety training before setting foot on a site, and you work with our leadership team from the written estimate through the final walkthrough. You work hard for every dollar and we work just as hard to save you every one we can, with transparent pricing, honest schedules and questions answered by our team members who work directly with you.

Sunny Singh

Sunny left Punjab as a young man to support his parents and siblings back home. He spent the next twenty years in the building trade in Brescia, Italy. In 2007, he brought his family to New York and founded SJNY Construction, which he has run day to day ever since, now nearly forty years into the craft.

Off the job, he watches soccer, rooting for Italy and the United States, and volunteers at his local Sikh temple, preparing food and clothing for donation.

Danny Singh

Danny started visiting his father's job sites in 2014 and grew into the trade from there. He earned a dual degree in architectural and construction engineering at Farmingdale State College while still working on those sites. He manages projects from first estimate through final sign-off.

Away from work, you will find him playing or watching sports and keeping his family and friends laughing.
