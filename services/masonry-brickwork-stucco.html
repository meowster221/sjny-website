How we approach it.

Masonry is the craft our firm was built on. Sunny learned it across twenty years in Brescia, Italy, and it shows in the joints: mortar matched to the original, brick toothed in rather than patched over, stone dressed to the profile it had a century ago.

Stucco gets the same discipline. We build the system in proper coats over sound substrate and finish it uniformly, so the facade sheds water and holds its color instead of cracking in its first winter. Where a wall has failed we find out why before we repair it.

Licensed general contractor. Fully insured. OSHA-trained crews. Serving the tri-state area since 2007.
