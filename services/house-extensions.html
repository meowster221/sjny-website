House Extensions

How we approach it

A good extension is the one you cannot pick out from the street. We engineer the new structure to carry its loads correctly, tie the roofline and facade into the original, and match finishes.

We coordinate the plans with your architect or engineer and handle the permits and inspections that extensions demand in New York. Most of our extension clients stay in the house through construction.

Licensed general contractor. Fully insured. OSHA-trained crews. Serving the tri-state area since 2007.
