House Extensions.

Horizontal and vertical additions engineered to carry new loads and read as part of the original structure, not as an afterthought.

Request an Estimate Call (347) 659-0881

How we approach it.

An extension succeeds when you cannot tell where the old house ends. We engineer the new structure to carry its loads correctly, tie the roofline and facade into the original, and match finishes so the addition reads as if it were always there.

We coordinate the plans with your architect or engineer and handle the permits and inspections that extensions demand in New York, and we keep the rest of your home livable while the work proceeds. Most of our extension clients stay in the house through construction.

Licensed general contractor. Fully insured. OSHA-trained crews. Serving the tri-state area since 2007.
