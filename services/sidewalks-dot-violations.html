How we approach it.

A DOT sidewalk violation does not age well, and neither does the trip hazard behind it. We replace the flags to city specification, protect the trees, and prepare and file the paperwork that supports the violation's dismissal from your record.

Most sidewalk jobs are measured in days, not weeks. We schedule tightly, pour to spec and leave the block cleaner than we found it.
