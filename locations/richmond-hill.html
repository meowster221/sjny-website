Building in Richmond Hill

Richmond Hill is a neighborhood of frame houses, brick semi-detached homes and mixed-use buildings along Liberty, Jamaica and Atlantic Avenues, and it asks for a contractor who has done all three. Our crews are on these blocks every week: rear and second-story extensions on houses that families are growing into, kitchens and bathrooms taken down to the studs, and driveways and stoops rebuilt on properly compacted bases.

Because we are based here, the paperwork moves too. We file with the Department of Buildings, close out DOT sidewalk violations and DOB violations, and stay on the job until the record is clean. You get one point of contact from the written estimate through the final walkthrough.

Licensed general contractor. Fully insured. OSHA-trained crews. Serving the tri-state area since 2007.
