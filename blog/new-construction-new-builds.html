New Construction & New Builds

New construction is not one job - it is dozens of jobs that have to happen in the right order. Good coordination is what keeps a project moving instead of having one trade waiting on another.

S&J NY Construction Inc. works with owners, developers, architects and engineers on residential and commercial construction projects.

Depending on the plans, our scope can include site preparation, excavation, footings, foundations, concrete, CMU, masonry, brick façades, framing, roofing, windows and doors, stucco, drywall, flooring, kitchens, bathrooms, painting, sidewalks, pavers and final finishes.

We are comfortable working directly from construction drawings and coordinating field conditions as the project moves forward. Our focus is on keeping the work practical, organized and built according to the approved plans and specifications.

If you are planning a new build, send us the drawings, project address and basic scope. We can review the project and discuss where S&J NY Construction can fit into the job.

Need an estimate? Send photos, drawings or the project scope and we will review the work with you.
