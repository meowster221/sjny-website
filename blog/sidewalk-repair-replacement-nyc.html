Sidewalk Repair & Replacement in NYC

Cracked and uneven sidewalks are common in New York, but once a section starts lifting, settling or breaking apart, it can quickly become a safety issue.

S&J NY Construction Inc. repairs and replaces concrete sidewalks for private homes, apartment buildings, commercial properties and managed buildings across NYC.

Our work includes damaged flag replacement, trip-hazard correction, cracked concrete removal, curb work, driveway aprons and DOT-related sidewalk repairs.

When we replace a section, we do more than remove the old concrete and pour new material. The area has to be prepared and graded correctly so the new work ties into the surrounding sidewalk cleanly and drains as it should.

If your sidewalk is already showing movement or heavy cracking, fixing it before the problem gets worse can save time and avoid a larger repair later.

Need an estimate? Send photos, drawings or the project scope and we will review the work with you.
