Asphalt Paving & Parking Lot Repair

Potholes and cracked asphalt are more than an appearance problem. Once water gets into the pavement and base, the damaged area usually keeps growing.

S&J NY Construction Inc. handles asphalt paving and repairs for parking lots, driveways and commercial properties.

Depending on the condition, the right solution may be patching, milling and resurfacing, crack repair or complete removal and replacement. We look at the existing pavement and base before recommending how far the repair should go.

Our asphalt work includes paving, parking lots, driveways, pothole repair, patching, milling, resurfacing and base repairs.

If your lot has widespread cracking, low areas or repeated potholes, send us photos or schedule a site visit. We can help determine whether a repair or a larger resurfacing project makes more sense.

Need an estimate? Send photos, drawings or the project scope and we will review the work with you.
