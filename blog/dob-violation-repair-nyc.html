DOB Violation Repair in NYC

Getting a DOB violation can be frustrating, especially when the notice uses technical language and it is not immediately clear what needs to be repaired.

The first step is always to look at the actual violation and understand the condition that was cited. From there, the repair may involve masonry, concrete, façade work, stucco, waterproofing, interior construction or another trade.

S&J NY Construction Inc. performs the physical construction work needed for many types of DOB violation corrections. We regularly work on brick replacement, pointing, parapet repairs, lintels, concrete repairs, stucco, fire escape preparation and other building repairs.

When an architect, engineer or other licensed professional is required, we work from their scope and complete the construction portion of the job. We do not guess at the violation; we want the repair to match what the building actually needs.

If you have an open violation, send us the violation number, notice, photos or engineer's report. We can review it and tell you what construction work we can handle.

Need an estimate? Send photos, drawings or the project scope and we will review the work with you.
